Analysis in Motion

Analysis in Motion is transforming orthopedic research by capturing the true patient journey – beyond the clinic and into the real world. Our mobile app, OrthoSteps, empowers researchers and research participants to follow post-injury progress using smartphone mobility data from Apple Health and Google Health Connect. Founded by leading clinicians and researchers – Nathan O'Hara PhD, MHA, Brian Shear, MD, Gerard Slobogean, MD, MPH (University of Maryland), and Dane Brodke, MD, MPH (Oregon Health & Science University) – we're leveraging smartphone data to proactively improve outcomes, predict complications, and refine treatments.

OrthoSteps App

OrthoSteps is a specialized steps and gait tracking app for participants in orthopaedic research studies.
