Analysis in Motion

We measure how patients recover in daily life, not just in the clinic. Our app, OrthoSteps, gives orthopaedic researchers objective mobility data from participants' smartphones through Apple Health and Google Health Connect, so they can track recovery, predict complications, and refine treatment.

Founded by Nathan O'Hara, PhD, MHA, and Gerard Slobogean, MD, MPH (University of California, Irvine); Brian Shear, MD (University of Maryland); and Dane Brodke, MD, MPH (Oregon Health & Science University).

OrthoSteps App

Step and gait tracking for participants in orthopaedic research studies.

Our Research

Our work in JBJS, JBJS Open Access, and JAAOS shows that smartphone mobility data can measure fracture recovery, predict function after injury, and inform weight-bearing and discharge decisions.
